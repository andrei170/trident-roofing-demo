Contact — what this page should look like

Your current contact page is 41 words. Your privacy policy is 721, which makes it the second longest page on your site. The contact page is the page someone reads once they have already decided they might use you — it is the last thing standing between an enquiry and a closed tab.

What it should answer

Who picks up the phone. What hours. How fast you normally come out. Whether the quote is free. Whether you charge to look. What happens after you have been. What areas you cover. Whether you handle the scaffold. Whether you do emergencies at night.

What it costs you to add

Nothing but an afternoon. Every one of those is something you already answer on the phone five times a week. Writing them down once means the ones who would not have called still get the answer.
